Behavior summary

What the banner does

  • Shows on first visit (no localStorage key found)
  • "Accept" → saves preference, loads gtag.js, hides banner
  • "Refuz" → saves preference, blocks gtag.js, hides banner
  • "Setări cookie-uri" footer link → reopens banner to change choice
  • Choice persists across visits via localStorage
  • All text in Romanian
  • Styled to match site: dark bg (#212529), gold accent (#ffc800)
  • Website still works fully without cookies

What it does NOT include

  • No cookie categories/preferences (simple binary choice)
  • No third-party consent management platform
  • No auto-blocking of other scripts (only gtag.js)
  • No privacy policy page (can be added separately)

Which banner style fits best?

A

Fixed Bottom Bar

Compact, non-blocking bar fixed to the bottom. Shows message + Accept/Reject buttons. Matches the dark footer style. Least intrusive, most common pattern.

B

Centered Modal Overlay

Blocks interaction until choice is made. Full GDPR compliance (explicit consent before interaction). More disruptive but legally safest. Matches Bootstrap modal style.

C

Slide-in Toast (Corner)

Small popup in bottom-right corner. Minimal, but may not meet GDPR requirements for explicit consent. Works well for non-EU visitors.
